Problem Statement

Accurate ETA prediction in Kolkata traffic is challenging due to:

  • Variable traffic patterns: Congestion varies by time-of-day, day-of-week, and route variant
  • GPS noise: Device inaccuracy and signal loss introduce jitter
  • Route complexity: Morning and evening have distinct patterns and chokepoints
  • Limited context: Weather, events, and incidents not yet captured
Goal: Build a regression model that predicts remaining ETA (minutes) from current position, time, speed, and route context.

Data Processing Pipeline

1. GPS Snapping

  • Project raw GPS points onto route baseline polyline
  • Compute nearest perpendicular foot (snapped position)
  • Record on-route progress and off-route distance

2. Elastic Redistribution

  • Enforce monotonic progress (no backward jumps)
  • Smooth local clumping at merge/diverge sections
  • Reduce teleport-like artifacts

3. Feature Engineering

  • Progress ratio: (meters traveled) / (total route meters)
  • Speed context: local + global bin-level median speeds
  • Time-of-day: departure minute in 24-hour clock
  • Weekday, vehicle class, off-route distance
    • weekday Categorical 0–6 (Monday–Sunday)

Next Steps to Improve Accuracy

Short-term (Feature & Regularization)

  • Feature diagnostics: Compute correlation & importance; identify weak/redundant features
  • Regularization sweep: Grid-search RF/XGB hyperparameters (max_depth, min_samples_leaf, lambda)
  • Route variant tagging: Explicitly label morning/evening route variants for finer segment modeling

Medium-term (Data & Context)

  • Collect more sessions: Expand coverage to rare traffic states and edge cases
  • Add calendar context: Holiday flags, event days, weekday/weekend patterns
  • Lagged features: Recent congestion trends (last 2–5 bins) to model queue propagation

Long-term (Modeling)

  • Hierarchical Bayesian ETA: Produce uncertainty intervals, not point predictions
  • Ensemble stacking: Combine LightGBM + XGBoost + Time-series models
  • Segment-level models: Predict per-bin travel time, then sum for full route
